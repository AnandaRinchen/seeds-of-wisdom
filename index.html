i. A life devoted

Khenpo
Kunga Rinchen

Born into the high plateaus of Kham, Khenpo Kunga Rinchen is a senior monk and scholar of the Sakya tradition — a teacher whose life has been quietly devoted to the Dharma and to the children who come to learn beneath the snow line.

He completed the monumental task of editing the 116-volume Gyud She Kuntu collection of teachings, and was unanimously appointed Khenpo by the monastic assembly of Rela Monastery — a recognition of decades of unwavering practice, scholarship, and care for his community.

Today he carries a sacred responsibility: managing the finances of the monastery's primary school, where dozens of young monks live, study, and grow.

ii. The Place

Among the
highest peaks on Earth

The school sits at the edge of glaciers — a small constellation of buildings cradled by snow and stone. Winters are long and unforgiving. Resources are few. But beneath the prayer flags, life is luminous.

Here, children rise before dawn to recite, to study, to learn the kindness that sustains the world.

iii. The Children

A forest of
compassion, one seed at a time

Robes of deep maroon and bright saffron. Faces still young, eyes already steady. These are the children whose education is supported by your kindness — sons of yak herders, of farmers, of families for whom schooling would otherwise be a distant prayer.

“Your contribution, no matter the size, helps us provide warm meals, books, robes, and a safe place to learn and grow.” — Khenpo Kunga Rinchen

iv. Letters of Recognition

Endorsed by the
highest masters of the lineage

Three letters of formal recognition — from His Holiness the Sakya Trizin, from the Rela Monastery assembly, and from the Elder Abbot of Ngor Luding — affirming Khenpo Kunga Rinchen's role and inviting your support.

SK Letter I · 2019

From Sakya Trizin

His Holiness the 41st Sakya Trizin

Confirms the conferral of the title of Khenpo upon the devoted monk Kunga Rinchen, and entrusts him with the sacred responsibility of managing the finances of the monastery's primary school. An appeal to all faithful and generous individuals.

RM Letter II

From the Monastery

Rela Monastery, Nangchen, Qinghai

Announces the unanimous nomination of Kunga Rinchen as Khenpo, recognizing the completion of his editing of the 116-volume Gyud She Kuntu, his long-standing dedication, and his proven ability to lead the community.

NL Letter III

From Luding Khenchen

Elder Abbot of Ngor Luding, Dehradun, India

A heartfelt commendation appealing to all benevolent individuals to extend their support and cooperation toward the school's needs — with the assurance that every contribution will be used judiciously and with utmost accountability.
